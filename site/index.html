Scheff for Zello

Zello push-to-talk firmware for ESP32 handhelds.

Scheff turns an ESP32 handheld into a pocket Zello radio. It signs in to your Zello account over Wi-Fi, joins a channel, and carries voice both ways through the board's own microphone and speaker, along with the channel's text messages. No phone and no extra hardware.

Flash

Needs desktop Chrome or Edge (Web Serial). Needs a secure (https) page.

What it does

  • Push-to-talk on Zello. Hold the big button (or the board's talk button) to talk. It turns green when the channel is yours and red with the talker's name while someone else has it.
  • Text messages. The channel's messages scroll in the activity panel, and you can type and send your own from the device or the browser.
  • Set up from the screen or a browser. Every setting is on the touch screen and on a built-in web page. Change it in one and the other follows.
  • Your own channel list. Favorites go to the top, then channels by how often you join them.
  • Screensaver. A schefflera bounces round the screen, showing who is talking. Audio keeps playing, and a pocket can't wake it by accident.
  • Backup and restore. One YAML file holds every setting and the channel list. Download it, or write it to a microSD card.
  • Crash and disconnect reports go to microSD, so problems can be traced.

Supported devices

LilyGO T-Display-P4

ESP32-P4 with a 568×1232 AMOLED. Talk with the on-screen button; BOOT is the screen button.

LilyGO T-Deck / T-Deck Plus

ESP32-S3 with keyboard and trackball. Hold the trackball click to talk, or press Space to start and again to stop.

Seeed Wio Tracker L2

ESP32-S3 with a touch screen. Hold the GPIO0 button or tap Talk; Wake is the screen button.

Nicolai Electronics Tanmatsu

ESP32-P4 with a full keyboard. Listen and text only, as it has no microphone. Replaces the badge.team launcher.

Connecting your Zello account

Every step works on the device's screen or in a browser, and each one follows the other.

  1. Flash and power on

    Install the firmware with the button above. On first boot the device starts an open Wi-Fi hotspot called Scheff-XXXX.

  2. Open the setup page

    Join the Scheff-XXXX hotspot from your phone or computer and go to http://192.168.4.1. There is no captive portal, so nothing pops up on its own; type in the address.

  3. Put it on your Wi-Fi

    Pick your network from the scan, or type its name, and enter the password. The device joins, saves it and restarts. The hotspot closes, and from then on the setup page is at http://<device-ip>. The address is shown at the bottom of every setup screen.

  4. Get a Zello developer token

    The public Zello network (zello.io) only lets third-party clients sign in with a developer token, a long JWT. Sign in at developers.zello.com with your Zello account and generate a token there. Skip this step for Zello Work.

  5. Enter the account

    In Zello account on the setup page:

    Zello (zello.io)Zello Work
    NetworkZello (zello.io)Zello Work
    Username / passwordYour Zello loginYour Zello Work login
    Developer tokenPaste the whole tokenNot used
    Network nameNot usedYour network's subdomain

    Paste the token in the browser. It is far too long to type on the device. Once you save, the device signs in to Zello straight away.

  6. Pick a channel

    Zello has no public channel directory, so add the channels you want by name. Names must match exactly, including spaces and capitals. Channels you join successfully are saved to the list. Then hold the button and talk.

Token expired?

Development tokens expire (typically after 30 days). When Zello refuses the sign-in, the device shows the token's expiry date and says to get a new one. Paste the new token in the setup page; leaving the field blank keeps the saved one.

Your credentials

The password and token stay on the device. The setup page never sends them back to the browser; it only shows whether one is saved. A backup file does contain them, unencrypted, so keep it somewhere safe.

Using it

  • Zello is half duplex: while someone else is talking, pressing to talk does nothing.
  • Turn on tap to talk in the web page's Audio section to press once to start and again to stop.
  • Activity (who joined, who is talking, messages, errors) scrolls on screen and under Channel activity in the browser. An incoming message beeps.
  • After 2 minutes untouched the screensaver comes up, and after 10 more the screen goes dark. Hold the screen button for 2 seconds to get back.
